02 / BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Appeal rules

01

Be truthful

False statements, altered evidence, or impersonation may result in immediate denial.

02

One active case

Do not submit duplicate appeals for the same platform and moderation action.

03

Stay respectful

Threats, harassment, or abusive language will not help an appeal and may close the case.

04

Decisions stay separate

An approval for one platform or punishment does not reverse decisions anywhere else.
